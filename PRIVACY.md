# The RatioJuris Privacy Promise

**Your device. Your data. Your control.**

At RatioJuris, we believe that your terminal sessions, credentials and private keys belong to you — not to us.

Droid Terminal is built with a privacy-first philosophy.

### Our Promise

- **No Analytics:** We do not integrate analytics or tracking services to monitor your application usage.
- **No Advertisements:** We do not display advertisements or use advertising SDKs.
- **No Developer-Controlled Servers:** Droid Terminal does not require RatioJuris-operated servers to function or store your terminal data.
- **Local Data Storage:** Your connection profiles, credentials and sensitive information are stored locally on your device.
- **Secure Vault:** Your SSH private keys, credentials and other sensitive information are protected by an encrypted local vault, designed to require authentication independent of your device's default screen lock.
- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, process or analyse your personal application data for our own purposes.

### A Commitment for the Future

This is not just a feature of the current release; it is a development commitment.

RatioJuris does not intend to introduce analytics, advertising, tracking, developer-operated data collection or mandatory cloud storage in future releases.

We intend to preserve Droid Terminal as a privacy-first, locally controlled application.

### Open Source. Open Commitment.

Droid Terminal is released under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (GPL-3.0-or-later).

You can inspect the source code, review how your information is handled, report security concerns and contribute improvements.

We welcome community participation and independent security reviews.

### Support Independent Development

Droid Terminal is developed with the aim of keeping your tools private, transparent and under your control.

You can support this commitment by:

- Contributing code and improvements.
- Reporting bugs and security vulnerabilities.
- Reviewing the source code.
- Sharing the project.
- Supporting development through voluntary donations.

**Privacy should not be a premium feature.**

Thank you for supporting independent, open-source software.
